# UK privacy and validation

Public data can still be personal data. Before operating with clients, document
the research purpose, controller/processor roles and a lawful basis. If relying
on legitimate interests, record a necessity/balancing assessment and safeguards.
Provide an Article 14 notice within the applicable period (normally one month)
unless a documented exception applies. Explain sources, profiling, retention,
rights, objection and deletion; supply a human review route. Complete a DPIA
screen for scale/profiling and check provider contracts, platform terms, UK data
transfers and access controls. This skill is not a legal compliance certificate.

Keep public professional handles, profile links, minimal niche text, dates,
verified counts, public post previews and declared business email only. An email
is business contact only when explicitly offered for work/enquiries. Do not guess
emails, collect private/contact-book data, phone numbers, home addresses, exact
coordinates, sensitive traits, follower lists or children's profiles. Do not keep
raw bios/datasets just because an actor returned them. Strip incidental personal
contact data before persistence and exports. Do not use public availability as
consent to email or DM. This feature sends no messages. PECR treats sole traders
and some partnerships like individuals; any later outreach needs a separate lawful
basis/consent review and suppression checks. Mere discovery is no content-use licence.

Monthly refresh is a calendar month after last successful official check, not a
startup-wide crawl. Use the current budget, deduplication and exclusion controls.
Default retention review is 90 days after last research use; delete expired,
unshortlisted records. Saved creators need an explicit continuing business purpose
review every 90 days. Delete creator removes profile/post history and segment links;
retain only a minimal hashed-handle suppression record if necessary to prevent
recollection after an objection. Suppression is still personal data; no marketing
use. Delete provider datasets after projection where supported. Never log content,
credentials, dataset URLs or provider error messages.

The owner labels `research/creator-briefs/test-set.csv`: handle,good_fit,is_uk,notes,
with yes/no labels only. Keep it header-only until real independent labels exist.
Match each label to the current frozen brief, rule version and saved creator result.
Unknown/missing predictions stay visible as coverage gaps. Report good-fit and UK
precision separately on Accuracy, with denominators and 95% Wilson intervals.
Both require >=85%, at least 20 predicted positives each, complete matched labels
and a real-owner source before client use. The 20-positive minimum is an initial
measurement safeguard, not evidence of statistical certainty. Report it explicitly.
Fixtures/sample data never open that gate. Changing rules or the brief requires a
new labelled validation. Ad tag and prediction gates in ACCURACY-SPEC stay unchanged.
