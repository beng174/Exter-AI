Which hooks are competitors scaling this month, and should we copy any of them?

Two hooks are being scaled hardest: offer-led and demo. Halvane has copied its "Refill and save" offer into 5 ads and kept it running for 64 days [1]. Demo hooks win in 44% of the category ads that use them, yet only 4% of your ads use one [2].

Founder-led openers are the fastest riser: Corrie & Moss launched 6 this week and they are already reaching people quickly [3].

Worth testing: a timed burn-test demo. It matches the second most common customer need, how long the candle lasts, raised in 71 reviews [4]. Copying Halvane's refill offer adds less: your own refill ad already wins, and offer-led hooks return below your benchmark.

Not checked: competitors' sales or ROAS (not public), and TikTok ads (library not connected).
